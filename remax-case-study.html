Remax Furnitures — Designed for Living

Six product photos, one brand voice, three distinct moods — a social campaign of nine Instagram carousels whose covers line up into a single seamless shopfront on the grid.

01The Brief

Remax Furnitures handed over six product photographs — an armchair, a sofa, a writing desk, a wing chair, a sculptural console and a marble side table — and one ask: make them feel like a brand, not a catalogue.

So I built three art directions from the same six pieces, wrote the on-image copy, and laid out nine Instagram carousels plus a festive story — designed so the covers snap together into one continuous scene on the profile grid.

02One shoot, three moods

Homely. A lived-in room — soft daylight, curtains, greenery and a patterned rug. The furniture is styled the way you'd actually keep it, so the buyer pictures it in their own home.

Studio. A clean white set and an elegant serif wordmark. Every piece lined up and evenly lit — the crisp, catalogue-grade view that lets the craftsmanship speak.

Cinematic. Golden-hour light rakes across the wall in hard window shadows. Bold, high-contrast and premium — the mood-first frame that stops the scroll.

Grid trick

Three posts that read as one

Each carousel's cover is one-third of a wider scene — posted in order they line up across the profile row into one storefront. This preview cycles all three vibes; hover to pause, or toggle the covers apart.

04A festive corner

To ride the season, I designed a Diwali story — the cane armchair lit by diyas, marigold garlands and a brass urli of floating flames. Warm, intimate, and unmistakably on-brand.

"Every corner tells a story — make yours unforgettable."

05What I Learned

One asset set, many stories

The same six photos can feel homely, clinical or cinematic — direction, light and type do more work than new photography ever could.

Design for the grid, not the post

Planning the covers as one panorama turns a profile into a storefront — a small constraint that makes the whole feed look intentional.
