02 — The method

AI does the lifting. I do the directing.

01

Ideate & plan

Brainstorm directions, pressure-test ideas, and structure the problem with Claude before anything gets designed.

02

Generate assets

Spin up images, scenes and mockups with ChatGPT — then art-direct, cut out and composite them to fit the brief.

03

Motion & video

Generate motion and video with Gemini, then edit and sync it into scroll experiences and reels.

04

Build & ship

Design and build the real thing with Claude Code — layout, interactions, polish — until it's production-ready.

The human layer is the whole point. Taste, art-direction, the final 20% of polish, and knowing when the AI is wrong — that's mine. AI makes me faster; it doesn't make the decisions.
