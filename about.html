Who I Am

Designer.
Thinker. Builder.

I'm Siddhant Gaikwad, a UI/UX Designer and Graphic Designer based in Ambernath, Maharashtra. I'm currently in my final year of BE Information Technology at A.P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane.

My design journey began with graphic design — building visual identities, social media content, and campaign creatives for clients. Over time, I fell deeply in love with the why behind design, which led me into UI/UX.

"Good design solves problems. Great design makes people feel something."

Today, I bring together visual craft and user empathy to design digital products that aren't just aesthetically beautiful, but deeply intuitive. I believe design should reduce friction, build trust, and create delight — all at once.

I'm currently working on three projects: a Spotify redesign, a food delivery website, and SmartSanstha — a gamified platform to make the Indian Constitution accessible and engaging for everyone.

How I Work

My design
process

Every great product starts with understanding people. Here's how I approach design.

01

Discover

Research users, understand pain points, define goals through interviews & analysis.

02

Define

Synthesize research into personas, journey maps, and clear problem statements.

03

Ideate

Sketch, wireframe, and explore multiple solutions before committing to a direction.

04

Prototype

Build interactive Figma prototypes that bring the design to life for testing.

05

Test & Iterate

Validate with users, gather feedback, and refine until it feels effortless.
