Who I am

Designer. Thinker.
Builder.

I'm Siddhant Gaikwad, a UI/UX and graphic designer based in Ambernath, Maharashtra — in my final year of BE Information Technology at A.P. Shah Institute of Technology, Thane.

My journey began in graphic design — visual identities, social media content, and campaign creatives for clients. Over time I fell for the why behind design, which pulled me into UI/UX.

Today I bring together visual craft and user empathy to design digital products that are beautiful and deeply intuitive. I believe design should reduce friction, build trust, and create delight — all at once.
